Tentang halaman iniAbout this page
  • Hasil disimpan saat panitia menutup lomba atau putaran latihan.
  • Peserta tampil dengan nomor peserta dan sekolah — tanpa nama anak.
  • Nilai = poin yang diraih dari poin maksimal; waktu = lama mengerjakan.
  • Peringkat ganda berarti nilai dan waktunya sama persis.
  • Results are saved when the organisers close a competition or a practice round.
  • Participants are shown by participant number and school — never a child's name.
  • Score = points earned out of the maximum; time = how long the paper took.
  • A shared rank means the score and the time were exactly equal.
