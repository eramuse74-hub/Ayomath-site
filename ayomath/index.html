AyoMath adalah aplikasi latihan matematika untuk anak SD Kelas 1–6 di Indonesia. Dirancang sebagai proyek sosial: gratis, tanpa iklan, dan bisa dipakai tanpa internet — agar bisa dipakai di mana saja, termasuk di daerah dengan jaringan terbatas.

AyoMath is a maths practice app for primary-school children in Grades 1–6 in Indonesia. Designed as a social project: free, ad-free, and usable without internet — so it works anywhere, including areas with limited connectivity.

📚 Sesuai Kurikulum MerdekaKelas 1–6, dari berhitung dasar sampai pecahan, desimal, geometri, dan soal cerita bertingkat. Follows Kurikulum MerdekaGrades 1–6, from basic arithmetic to fractions, decimals, geometry and multi-step word problems.
📶 Bekerja offlineProgres belajar tersimpan di HP. Tidak butuh akun, tidak butuh kuota. Works offlineLearning progress is saved on the phone. No account, no data plan needed.
🛡️ Aman & menghormati privasiTanpa iklan, tanpa pelacak, tanpa data pribadi anak. Ada batas waktu bermain & Area Orang Tua. Safe & privacy-respectingNo ads, no trackers, no personal data from children. Includes a play-time limit & a Parent Area.
⭐ Belajar nilai baikSetiap petualangan menyelipkan nilai: kebersihan, kejujuran, hormat, dan kebaikan hati. Learning good valuesEvery adventure weaves in a value: cleanliness, honesty, respect and kindness.

Mode & Fitur

Modes & Features

Lebih dari sekadar latihan. AyoMath punya banyak mode — dan semua mulai dari yang mudah, lalu makin menantang seiring kemampuan anak bertambah, jadi selalu pas dengan levelnya.

More than just drills. AyoMath has many modes — and all of them start easy, then grow more challenging as the child's ability grows, so it always matches their level.

🚀 PetualanganJalur belajar utama tanpa batas. Anak naik level dan menjelajah Dunia demi Dunia; soal bertambah sulit secara bertahap mengikuti kemajuannya. Kumpulkan XP & stiker koleksi. AdventureThe main, endless learning path. Children level up and explore World after World; problems get harder step by step as they progress. Collect XP & collectible stickers.
🪐 Belajar per KelasPilih Kelas 1–6 dan latih topik tertentu — penjumlahan, pengurangan, perkalian, pembagian, pecahan, desimal, geometri, sampai soal cerita. Learn by GradePick Grade 1–6 and practise a specific topic — addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, geometry, all the way to word problems.
🎲 Permainan AsikGame singkat yang mengasah logika & ketelitian: Pilah Cipi, Lari Ciko, Tangram Leo, Sudoku Tora, Lukis Bobo, dan Toko Oren. Tingkat kesulitannya naik saat anak makin mahir. Fun GamesShort games that sharpen logic & accuracy: Cipi's Cleanup, Ciko's Run, Leo's Tangram, Tora's Sudoku, Bobo's Painting and Oren's Shop. Difficulty rises as the child gets better.
⚡ Mode CepatLatih kecepatan berhitung di luar kepala. Target waktunya makin ketat untuk melatih kelancaran (fluency). Quick ModeTrain mental-arithmetic speed. The time target gets tighter to build fluency.
🧠 Mode Jenius — bertaraf internasionalSoal penalaran mendalam & non-rutin per kelas, dipatok (benchmark) ke standar Singapura & Korea — negara dengan prestasi matematika terbaik dunia. Memakai model bar, langkah bertingkat, dan berpikir mundur; kesulitannya terus meningkat. Raih Bintang Jenius ⭐ tanpa memengaruhi progres utama. Genius Mode — international standardDeep, non-routine reasoning problems for each grade, benchmarked against Singapore & Korea — the world's top-performing countries in maths. Uses bar models, multi-step working and working backwards; difficulty keeps rising. Earn Genius Stars ⭐ without affecting the main progress.
🏆 Papan JuaraAdu XP dengan teman sekelas, se-provinsi, dan seluruh Indonesia. Tanpa nama asli — tetap aman & privat. LeaderboardCompete on XP with classmates, your province and all of Indonesia. No real names — safe & private.
🛍️ Toko & ProfilBelanja topi & teman hewan dengan koin hasil bermain, lalu pantau kemampuan, level, dan pangkat anak di Profil. Shop & ProfileBuy hats & animal friends with coins earned by playing, then track the child's skills, level and rank in the Profile.
📈 Tumbuh bersama anakTidak ada mode yang “terlalu mudah” untuk selamanya. Dari berhitung dasar sampai penalaran ala Singapura/Korea di Mode Jenius, tingkat kesulitan naik bertahap — menantang tanpa membuat anak menyerah. Grows with the childNo mode stays “too easy” forever. From basic arithmetic to Singapore/Korea-style reasoning in Genius Mode, difficulty rises gradually — challenging without making children give up.
