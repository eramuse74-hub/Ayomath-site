Complex numbers and Euler's formula

The reframe this module is built from A complex number is a length and a direction. Multiplying rotates and scales. $e^{j\theta}$ is pure rotation. Everything below — powers, roots, the trig identities, phasors — is that one sentence wearing different clothes.

How this module is meant to be used

Attempt every question before touching a hint — sketch it, guess, commit. A wrong committed answer tells the site which wrong model you are holding, and it will answer that model rather than mark arithmetic. Hints come one level at a time and the next level only unlocks after you have tried again. The solution is level four; it exists, but the step you take yourself is the one that becomes yours.

A clean first solve is mastered. Anything you needed help on returns in three days and again after two weeks — a concept that is understood can be re-derived, and re-deriving gets fast. Export your progress from time to time; the browser will eventually clear it.
