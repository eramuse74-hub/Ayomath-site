Prerequisite mathematics refresh

What this module is for Nothing here is new. It is the algebra, trigonometry and calculus that ECE 230, BME 310 and module 1 assume you can do without thinking — cancelling correctly, reading an angle off the unit circle, the chain rule, isolating an exponential. Use it as a diagnostic: skip what is easy, stay on what is not. Everything is optional; nothing later depends on finishing it.

Figures you drive

The unit circle underlies every trig question; the quadratic figure shows roots leaving the real axis (module 1 starts exactly there); the tangent figure is what a derivative is.

How to use this module

  • Work through the list once, fast. Anything solved cleanly is mastered and will not come back.
  • Anything that needed a hint or a second attempt returns in three days and again after two weeks. That is the repair.
  • Skip what is easy. Jump to any item from the list on the right.
  • Read the background first if an idea itself is shaky. Each question links to its explainer.
  • Three sections: algebra (items 1–10), trigonometry (11–18), calculus reflexes (19–25). Module 1 assumes the trigonometry section in particular.
